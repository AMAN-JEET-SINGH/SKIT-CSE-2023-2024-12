# SentiCare 🧠❤️

### An AI Assistant for Psychological & Physical Well-Being

SentiCare is a multimodal AI-powered wellness assistant designed to help users
understand and monitor their emotional and physical well-being.

The system combines **facial expression analysis, voice emotion analysis, and
health metrics** to provide personalized wellness guidance, track well-being
trends, and support timely intervention when critical conditions are detected.

# 📌 Overview

People may experience stress, anxiety, fatigue, poor sleep, and other
emotional or physical challenges in their daily lives. These signals can often
go unnoticed when emotional and physical health are monitored separately.

SentiCare aims to address this gap through a **multimodal wellness system**
that brings together:

- Facial expression analysis
- Speech emotion analysis
- Health data
- AI-powered conversations
- Personalized wellness guidance
- Well-being trend tracking
- Risk assessment
- Alerts for parents, guardians, or trusted contacts

The goal is to provide users with a unified platform for understanding their
emotional and physical well-being.

---

# 🎯 Objectives

SentiCare aims to:

- Detect emotions using facial expressions.
- Analyze emotions from voice and speech.
- Integrate health metrics such as heart rate, SpO₂, blood pressure, sleep,
  and activity.
- Provide personalized AI-powered wellness guidance.
- Support supportive AI conversations.
- Track emotional and physical health trends over time.
- Identify potentially concerning states using multimodal information.
- Send alerts to trusted contacts when critical conditions are detected,
  subject to user consent.
- Promote preventive health and healthy lifestyle habits.
